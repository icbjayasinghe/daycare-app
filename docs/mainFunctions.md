
# Features and functions

## Main Actors 3
1. Admin
1. Daycare owner/representative
1. Parent

## Admin
1. Manage daycare registation requests 
1. Add, Modify, and Remove daycare metadata
1. *Review daycares and edit details*

## Daycare owner
1. Register daycare with the system
1. Create capacity of daycare either by,
    - Age
    - Age groups
1. Update availability for each age/ age group capacity
1. Add/mark available facilities and their operating times
1. Upload facility photos so parents can easily have a look
1. Receive notifications on kids completing age/ age group to leave daycare/ age level

## Parent
1. Register kids
1. View daycares
1. Select preferred daycares to apply
1. receiving alerts on updates
    - Upcoming vacant slots for age/age group on a daycare watchlist
    - When their kid approaching the end of a age/age group
    - *When a daycare facility closing time is reaching*
